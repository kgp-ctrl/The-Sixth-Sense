- Added a Debug button to reload only the eight HUD PNGs, with direct development-source reads, optional local PNG overrides, and validation before replacing existing textures.
- Added a localized, scrollable feature checklist through the i button below image reload.
- Fixed the info checklist being blurred by the default screen background pass after its text was drawn.
- Made targeting thorns appear over one second and shrink 12% while moving inward on impact; preserved the author's edited thorn PNG.
- Refined bow/crossbow wood grain with restrained oak/spruce colors and matte metal details.
- Enlarged the logo's eye, removed its inner ring and added a brighter stone background.
- Aligned the root license with All Rights Reserved metadata, while respecting prior licenses and permitting private gameplay edits.
- Moved authoring tools, previews, store drafts and historical backups outside the repository; kept build regression checks.
- Refined Shulker, Ghast, bow, and crossbow animations at 32-pixel frame resolution while preserving their compact HUD size.
- Made Ghast mouth movement follow charging and fireball release; cancelled charges recover without a firing pose.
- Gave the Shulker a mostly forward gaze, occasional side glances, and a spinning projectile on a smooth orbit.
- Kept the Warden heart centered in the automatic layout and arranged other warnings beside it.
- Smoothed the Warden compass and added a gentle breathing motion.
- Added matte thorn brackets with targeting feedback in singleplayer, confirmed Warden-hit feedback in multiplayer, inward impact movement, and health-dependent red tips.
- Preserved the author's explosion and Warden-heart textures.
- Updated release metadata, project documentation, store-page copy, and regression coverage.

The continuous Warden heartbeat, anchored HUD editor, 3× size compensation, and seven-language interface remain included.
